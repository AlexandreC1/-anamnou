## Design Context

### Creator direction, October 4, 2026

The creator explicitly requested a bold redesign with 3D graphics, modern glass,
color, and contemporary UX. This supersedes the earlier restrained terracotta
direction. Use a peach reading canvas, coral/lilac/sage sculptural objects,
selective translucent controls, and Bricolage Grotesque with Public Sans. Preserve
Literata for the actual yearbook publication. Keep the product warm, mature, and
collaborative, with real create/join paths and four-language support. The landing
cover is labeled as an illustration; authenticated class content remains real.
CSS perspective supplies the interactive 3D cover without a WebGL dependency.
Respect reduced motion, keyboard access, and narrow phone layouts.

### Users

The creator's Bible identifies graduating students, class representatives, staff,
school administrators, and invited guests. Phone use is central. The eventual job
is to preserve a class publication and revisit it as alumni.

### Brand Personality

Warm, editorial, celebratory (provided by the creator). Mature rather than childish.
Anamnou is the restored display name following the creator's latest direction.
The tagline is “Yon chapit fini. Yon istwa rete.” Infrastructure uses neutral yearbook identifiers.

### Aesthetic Direction

A publication on a light reading canvas: terracotta covers, compact navigation,
large book typography, generous white space. Light presentation suits phone reading.
Font exploration rejected reflex choices Playfair Display, Fraunces, and Inter.
Literata and Public Sans were selected from the Google Fonts catalog for book text
and clear interface labels, delivered locally with Fontsource, without CDN requests.

### Design Principles

- Mobile reading before decorative motion.
- Give headings space; avoid repetitive cards and fabricated content.
- Expose only functioning routes in the authorized phase.
- Visible keyboard focus, semantic landmarks, reduced motion support.
- Keep developer diagnostics on a separate connection page.

### Creator feedback, September 14, 2026

The interface needs more character and a professional finish. Use deliberate page
composition and real class content. Avoid generic AI styling, decorative symbols,
emoticons, and em dashes in product copy or communication.

The creator found the first green-sidebar design underwhelming. The second pass
centers the actual edition: a two-page opening spread, shared portraits and quotes,
consistent cover rendering, and a clickable section index in the editor. Keep
visual evaluation separate from passing engineering checks.
