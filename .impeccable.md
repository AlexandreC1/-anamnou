## Design Context

### Users

The creator's Bible identifies graduating students, class representatives, staff,
school administrators, and invited guests. Phone use is central. The eventual job
is to preserve a class publication and revisit it as alumni.

### Brand Personality

Warm, editorial, celebratory (provided by the creator). Mature rather than childish.
TÈLÒ is the selected wordmark, with Telos as its companion name.
The tagline is “Yon chapit fini. Yon istwa rete.” Infrastructure uses neutral yearbook identifiers.

### Aesthetic Direction

A printed opening page: paper-colored canvas, deep ink, restrained terracotta,
large book typography, generous white space. Light presentation suits phone reading.
Font exploration rejected reflex choices Playfair Display, Fraunces, and Inter.
Literata and Public Sans were selected from the Google Fonts catalog for book text
and clear interface labels, delivered locally with Fontsource, without CDN requests.

### Design Principles

- Mobile reading before decorative motion.
- Give headings space; avoid repetitive cards and fabricated content.
- Expose only functioning routes in Phase 0.
- Visible keyboard focus, semantic landmarks, reduced motion support.
- Keep developer diagnostics on a separate connection page.
